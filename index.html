    In Samsung Food: Saved → + → Save recipe link → paste one link at a time.

    Order on Walmart

    One-click cart fill uses Walmart's add-to-cart hand-off link. It needs Walmart's item ID for each product, so the first time through, click Find on Walmart, pick the product you like, and paste its page link into the box. The site saves the ID and reuses it every week.

    Add week to Walmart cart

    Walmart opens with the items in your cart; you still review quantities and check out there.

    Send to Samsung Food

    Samsung Food (and the Family Hub fridge) can order a shopping list through Walmart or Instacart. Two ways to get this week in there:

    1. Recipes: copy the recipe links, then in Samsung Food go to Saved → + → Save recipe link. Each page carries recipe markup so ingredients import cleanly. Add them to the Samsung Food shopping list and tap Shop → Walmart.
    2. List only: copy the list as one item per line and add the lines to a Samsung Food shopping list.
