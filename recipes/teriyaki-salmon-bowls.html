Teriyaki salmon bowls with edamame

Dinner · serves 4 · 30 min total, 15 min hands-on · bowls · omega-3

560 kcal42 g protein62 g carbs16 g fat

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb Salmon fillets
  • 4 tbsp Low-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Honey
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tsp Ground ginger
  • 1 tbsp Cornstarch
  • 12 oz Frozen shelled edamame
  • 2 cups Jasmine rice (dry)
  • 1 each Cucumber
  • 1 bunch Green onions

Steps

  1. Start 2 cups rice. Heat oven to 400°F.
  2. Simmer soy, honey, garlic, ginger, cornstarch and 1/3 cup water 2 minutes until it thickens into teriyaki.
  3. Brush salmon with half the sauce, bake 12–14 minutes. Microwave edamame per the bag.
  4. Build 4 bowls: rice, salmon, edamame, sliced cucumber, green onion. Drizzle remaining sauce.
