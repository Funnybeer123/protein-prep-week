Mediterranean tuna & chickpea salad

Lunch · serves 4 · 15 min total, 15 min hands-on · no cook · eat cold

380 kcal40 g protein24 g carbs13 g fat

Ingredients

  • 4 cans Canned tuna in water
  • 1 can Chickpeas (can)
  • 1 each Cucumber
  • 1 pint Grape tomatoes
  • 3 oz Crumbled feta
  • 1 each Lemons
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning

Steps

  1. Drain tuna and chickpeas. Dice cucumber, halve tomatoes.
  2. Toss everything with lemon juice, oil, Italian seasoning, salt and pepper.
  3. Portion into 4 containers. Keeps 4 days.
