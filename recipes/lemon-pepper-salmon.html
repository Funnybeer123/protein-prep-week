Lemon-pepper salmon, asparagus & baby potatoes

Dinner · serves 4 · 35 min total, 10 min hands-on · sheet pan · omega-3

480 kcal38 g protein32 g carbs22 g fatestimate

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb Salmon fillets
  • 1 lb Asparagus
  • 1.5 lb Baby gold potatoes
  • 1 each Lemons
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 3 tsp Lemon pepper seasoning
  • 1 tsp Salt & black pepper

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Halve potatoes, toss with 1 tbsp oil and salt, roast 15 minutes.
  2. Push potatoes aside. Add salmon (cut into 4) and trimmed asparagus. Rub with remaining oil, garlic, lemon pepper.
  3. Roast 12 more minutes until salmon flakes. Squeeze lemon over everything.
  4. Portion into 4 containers. Reheat gently or eat salmon cold over the potatoes.
