Easy chicken curry with spinach & rice

Dinner · serves 4 · 35 min total, 20 min hands-on · one pot · freezer friendly

560 kcal46 g protein50 g carbs18 g fatestimate

Ingredients

  • 2 lb Boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 0.5 cups Nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp Curry powder
  • 4 cloves Garlic
  • 1 each Yellow onions
  • 1 can Fire-roasted diced tomatoes (can)
  • 1 can Light coconut milk (can)
  • 5 oz Baby spinach
  • 2 cups Jasmine rice (dry)
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil

Steps

  1. Start 2 cups rice. Cube chicken and toss with yogurt and 1 tbsp curry powder.
  2. Soften diced onion and garlic in oil, 4 minutes. Add chicken, cook 6 minutes.
  3. Add remaining curry powder, tomatoes and coconut milk. Simmer 12 minutes. Stir in spinach until wilted.
  4. Portion over rice into 4 containers.
