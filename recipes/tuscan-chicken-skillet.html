Tuscan chicken skillet with protein pasta

Dinner · serves 4 · 30 min total, 25 min hands-on · one pan · creamy

560 kcal60 g protein42 g carbs15 g fatestimate

Ingredients

  • 2 lb Boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 10 oz Baby spinach
  • 1 pint Grape tomatoes
  • 4 cloves Garlic
  • 1 cup Chicken broth
  • 0.5 cups Nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 oz Grated parmesan
  • 2 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 8 oz Chickpea or protein pasta
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil

Steps

  1. Boil pasta per the box. Cut chicken into bite-size pieces and season.
  2. Sear chicken in oil 6 minutes. Add garlic, halved tomatoes and Italian seasoning, 2 minutes.
  3. Add broth, simmer 3 minutes. Off the heat, stir in yogurt, parmesan and spinach until wilted.
  4. Toss with pasta. Portion into 4 containers.
