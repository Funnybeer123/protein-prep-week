Banana protein pancakes

Breakfast · serves 4 · 25 min total, 25 min hands-on · reheats well

400 kcal36 g protein50 g carbs6 g fat

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Old-fashioned oats
  • 1.5 cups Liquid egg whites
  • 2 scoops Whey protein powder
  • 2 each Bananas
  • 0.5 cups Nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 tsp Ground cinnamon

Steps

  1. Blend everything until smooth. Rest 5 minutes.
  2. Cook 1/4-cup pancakes on a nonstick pan over medium, 2 minutes per side. Makes about 12.
  3. Store 3 per container. Toast or microwave to reheat.
