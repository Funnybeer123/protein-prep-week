Slow-cooker BBQ pulled chicken, potatoes & slaw

Dinner · serves 4 · 240 min total, 15 min hands-on · slow cooker · dump & go

520 kcal56 g protein52 g carbs8 g fat

Ingredients

  • 2.5 lb Boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 8 tbsp BBQ sauce
  • 1 each Yellow onions
  • 2 lb Baby gold potatoes
  • 14 oz Coleslaw mix (shredded cabbage)
  • 0.5 cups Nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 each Limes
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Salt & black pepper

Steps

  1. Slow cooker: chicken, sliced onion, 6 tbsp BBQ sauce. HIGH 3–4 hours. Shred, stir in remaining sauce.
  2. Oven 425°F: halve potatoes, toss with oil and salt, roast 25 minutes.
  3. Slaw: toss coleslaw mix with yogurt, lime juice, salt and pepper.
  4. Portion chicken and potatoes into 4 containers; keep slaw in separate cups.
