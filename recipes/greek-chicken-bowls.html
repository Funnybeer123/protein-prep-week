Greek chicken bowls with tzatziki

Dinner · serves 4 · 35 min total, 20 min hands-on · bowls · eat cold

540 kcal50 g protein42 g carbs20 g fat

Ingredients

  • 2 lb Boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 2 each Lemons
  • 4 cloves Garlic
  • 3 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1.5 cups Quinoa (dry)
  • 1 each Cucumber
  • 1 pint Grape tomatoes
  • 4 oz Crumbled feta
  • 1 cup Nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 tsp Salt & black pepper

Steps

  1. Cook quinoa (1.5 cups quinoa, 3 cups water, 15 minutes). Heat oven to 425°F.
  2. Toss thighs with juice of 1 lemon, 3 garlic cloves, Italian seasoning, oil, salt. Roast 22 minutes. Slice.
  3. Tzatziki: grate half the cucumber, squeeze dry, stir into yogurt with 1 garlic clove, remaining lemon juice and salt.
  4. Build 4 bowls: quinoa, chicken, diced cucumber, halved tomatoes, feta. Pack tzatziki in small cups.
