Turkey taco bowls

Dinner · serves 4 · 30 min total, 20 min hands-on · one pot · bowls

560 kcal50 g protein55 g carbs15 g fat

Ingredients

  • 2 lb 93/7 lean ground turkey
  • 2 each Taco seasoning packet
  • 1 can Black beans (can)
  • 1 cup Salsa
  • 2 cups Jasmine rice (dry)
  • 4 oz Shredded cheddar
  • 1 each Romaine hearts
  • 1 each Limes

Steps

  1. Start 2 cups rice. Brown the turkey in a large skillet over medium-high, breaking it up, about 8 minutes.
  2. Stir in taco seasoning, 2/3 cup water, and the drained black beans. Simmer 5 minutes until thick.
  3. Chop romaine. Build 4 bowls: rice, turkey mix, salsa, cheddar. Pack lettuce in a separate bag so it stays crisp.
  4. Squeeze lime over each bowl before eating.
