Cottage cheese egg bites

Breakfast · serves 4 · 30 min total, 10 min hands-on · muffin tin · reheats well

280 kcal30 g protein6 g carbs14 g fatestimate

Ingredients

  • 8 each Large eggs
  • 1 cup Liquid egg whites
  • 1 cup Low-fat cottage cheese
  • 5 oz Baby spinach
  • 2 oz Shredded cheddar
  • 1 each Bell peppers
  • 1 tsp Salt & black pepper

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Grease a 12-cup muffin tin well.
  2. Blend eggs, egg whites, cottage cheese, salt and pepper. Stir in chopped spinach, diced pepper and cheddar.
  3. Fill cups, bake 22 minutes until set. Cool, store 3 per container. Microwave 45 seconds.
