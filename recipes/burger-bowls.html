Burger bowls with oven fries

Dinner · serves 4 · 35 min total, 15 min hands-on · bowls · crowd pleaser

540 kcal50 g protein32 g carbs24 g fatestimate

Ingredients

  • 2 lb 93/7 lean ground beef
  • 2 each Romaine hearts
  • 1 pint Grape tomatoes
  • 4 oz Shredded cheddar
  • 1 each Yellow onions
  • 1.5 lb Baby gold potatoes
  • 0.5 cups Nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp Sriracha
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 tsp Garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Salt & black pepper

Steps

  1. Oven 425°F: cut potatoes into wedges, toss with oil, salt and garlic powder. Roast 25 minutes.
  2. Brown beef with diced onion, salt and pepper, 8 minutes. Drain.
  3. Sauce: yogurt + sriracha. Chop romaine and halve tomatoes.
  4. Pack beef, cheddar and fries hot; lettuce, tomato and sauce cold in separate cups. Assemble when eating.
