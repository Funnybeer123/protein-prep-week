Egg roll in a bowl

Dinner · serves 4 · 25 min total, 20 min hands-on · one pan · low carb option

470 kcal41 g protein38 g carbs18 g fat

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb 93/7 lean ground beef
  • 14 oz Coleslaw mix (shredded cabbage)
  • 3 tbsp Low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp Toasted sesame oil
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 1 bunch Green onions
  • 1 tsp Ground ginger
  • 1 tbsp Sriracha
  • 1.5 cups Jasmine rice (dry)

Steps

  1. Start 1.5 cups rice. Brown beef in a large skillet with garlic and ginger, 7 minutes.
  2. Add the whole bag of coleslaw mix, soy sauce and sesame oil. Cook 4 minutes until cabbage wilts.
  3. Stir in sliced green onions and sriracha. Portion over rice into 4 containers.
