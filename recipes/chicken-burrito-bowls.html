Chicken burrito bowls

Lunch · serves 4 · 30 min total, 15 min hands-on · bowls

520 kcal42 g protein58 g carbs12 g fat

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb Boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 1.5 cups Jasmine rice (dry)
  • 1 can Black beans (can)
  • 12 oz Frozen sweet corn
  • 1 cup Salsa
  • 2 each Limes
  • 1 bunch Cilantro
  • 1 each Taco seasoning packet

Steps

  1. Start 1.5 cups rice. Dice chicken, toss with taco seasoning, sear 8 minutes.
  2. Stir lime juice and chopped cilantro into the cooked rice.
  3. Bowls: rice, chicken, drained beans, corn, salsa.
