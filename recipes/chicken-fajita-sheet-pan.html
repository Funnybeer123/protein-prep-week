Chicken fajita sheet pan

Dinner · serves 4 · 30 min total, 10 min hands-on · sheet pan · tacos

500 kcal52 g protein40 g carbs13 g fat

Ingredients

  • 2 lb Boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 3 each Bell peppers
  • 2 each Yellow onions
  • 2 each Taco seasoning packet
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 8 each High-protein or low-carb tortillas
  • 0.5 cups Nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 each Limes

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Slice chicken, peppers and onions into strips.
  2. Toss everything with oil and taco seasoning on a sheet pan. Roast 20 minutes, stirring once.
  3. Portion into 4 containers with 2 tortillas each (wrap separately). Greek yogurt stands in for sour cream.
  4. Finish with lime juice when eating.
