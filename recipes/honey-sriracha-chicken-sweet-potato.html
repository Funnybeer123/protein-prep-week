Honey-sriracha chicken & sweet potato sheet pan

Dinner · serves 4 · 35 min total, 10 min hands-on · sheet pan · one pan

520 kcal48 g protein48 g carbs16 g fatestimate

Ingredients

  • 2 lb Boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 2 lb Sweet potatoes
  • 0.75 lb Broccoli florets
  • 2 tbsp Honey
  • 2 tbsp Sriracha
  • 2 tbsp Low-sodium soy sauce
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Salt & black pepper

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Cube sweet potatoes, toss with 1 tbsp oil and salt, roast 10 minutes.
  2. Toss thighs with honey, sriracha, soy, garlic and remaining oil. Add to pan with broccoli. Roast 22 minutes.
  3. Portion into 4 containers, spooning the glaze over the top.
