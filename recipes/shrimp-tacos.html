Shrimp tacos with lime slaw

Dinner · serves 4 · 20 min total, 20 min hands-on · 20 min · tacos

490 kcal46 g protein40 g carbs15 g fat

Ingredients

  • 2 lb Raw shrimp, peeled & deveined
  • 1 each Taco seasoning packet
  • 14 oz Coleslaw mix (shredded cabbage)
  • 2 each Limes
  • 0.5 cups Nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 1 bunch Cilantro
  • 2 each Avocados
  • 12 each High-protein or low-carb tortillas
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil

Steps

  1. Thaw shrimp, pat dry, toss with taco seasoning. Sear in oil 2 minutes per side.
  2. Slaw: coleslaw mix, yogurt, juice of 2 limes, chopped cilantro, salt.
  3. Pack shrimp, slaw, tortillas (3 each) and half an avocado per container separately. Assemble when eating.
