Buffalo chicken quinoa bowls

Lunch · serves 4 · 30 min total, 15 min hands-on · bowls

480 kcal50 g protein35 g carbs14 g fatestimate

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb Boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 4 tbsp Buffalo wing sauce
  • 1 cup Quinoa (dry)
  • 1 each Romaine hearts
  • 1 pint Grape tomatoes
  • 0.5 cups Nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 oz Shredded cheddar

Steps

  1. Cook quinoa (1 cup quinoa, 2 cups water, 15 minutes).
  2. Cube chicken, sear 8 minutes, toss with buffalo sauce.
  3. Bowls: quinoa, chicken, chopped romaine, tomatoes, cheddar. Yogurt on the side as ranch-style cooling.
