Slow-cooker salsa chicken bowls

Dinner · serves 4 · 240 min total, 10 min hands-on · slow cooker · dump & go

540 kcal55 g protein55 g carbs10 g fatestimate

Ingredients

  • 2.5 lb Boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 2 cups Salsa
  • 1 each Taco seasoning packet
  • 1 can Black beans (can)
  • 2 cups Jasmine rice (dry)
  • 4 oz Shredded cheddar
  • 1 bunch Cilantro
  • 1 each Limes

Steps

  1. Put chicken, salsa and taco seasoning in the slow cooker. Cook on HIGH 3–4 hours or LOW 6.
  2. Shred chicken with two forks right in the pot. Stir in drained black beans.
  3. Start 2 cups rice 20 minutes before serving. Portion rice and chicken into 4 containers, top with cheddar and cilantro.
  4. Squeeze lime over each bowl when eating.
