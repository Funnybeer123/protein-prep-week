PB-banana smoothie packs

Breakfast · serves 4 · 10 min total, 10 min hands-on · no cook · freezer

450 kcal40 g protein48 g carbs12 g fat

Ingredients

  • 4 each Bananas
  • 4 scoops Whey protein powder
  • 4 tbsp Peanut butter
  • 1 cup Old-fashioned oats
  • 4 cups 2% milk

Steps

  1. Slice bananas into 4 freezer bags with 1/4 cup oats and 1 tbsp peanut butter each. Freeze.
  2. Morning: dump a bag in the blender with 1 cup milk and 1 scoop protein. Blend 30 seconds.
